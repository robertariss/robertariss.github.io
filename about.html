About Me

Intro

I am a physician-scientist interested in using artificial intelligence to improve patient care. I completed my undergraduate studies in bioengineering followed by a medical doctorate at the University of Toledo as part of the accelerated BS/MD program. Following this, I completed internal medicine residency at Brigham and Women’s Hospital and Harvard Medical School. I am currently a fellow in cardiovascular medicine at Columbia University, and I completing a Master of Science in Biostatistics at the Mailman School of Public Health.
